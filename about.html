About Eventzone.ng

A Lagos based team that treats your event like it is the only one on our calendar

We started in 2017 planning a single friend's wedding, and have since grown into a full service studio; the promise has not changed, we still plan every event as if it were the only one we had.

Our story

Seven years of learning what actually makes an event work

Eventzone.ng grew out of a simple frustration; too many good ideas for weddings and events fell apart at execution. We built a team that is equally strong at concept, budgeting and logistics, so the idea on the mood board is the thing your guests actually see.

Today we plan weddings, corporate functions and social celebrations for clients across Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, working with a vetted network of caterers, decorators, photographers and venues.

What we believe

The values that shape every event we run

Plan for the detail

We write the budget line by line and the run of show minute by minute, so surprises stay pleasant, not logistical.

One point of contact

You get a single planner who knows your event end to end, not a rotating cast answering the same questions twice.

Honest budgets
