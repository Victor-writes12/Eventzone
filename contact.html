Before you write in

A few common questions

How far ahead should I book?

For weddings and large corporate events, six to nine months gives us the best vendor availability; smaller social events can often be planned in four to six weeks.

Do you work outside Lagos?

Yes, we regularly plan events in Abuja and Port Harcourt, and take on destination events with advance notice.

Can I book decor without full planning?

Yes, our design and styling package works alongside your own planning team or vendors.

What is included in the first call?

A thirty minute conversation about your date, budget range and vision, followed by a written concept and quote.
