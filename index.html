What we do

Full service planning, without the guesswork

Weddings

Traditional, white or destination; we plan the timeline, manage vendors and hold the day together so your family can just be present.

Corporate events

Launches, conferences and staff functions delivered on brand, on budget and on time, with a run of show your team can trust.

Social celebrations

Anniversaries, naming and graduation events, styled to feel personal rather than generic, for gatherings of any size.

Adult celebrations

Milestone birthdays, retirement send offs and anniversary parties for the adults in your life; we style these with the same care and detail as a wedding.

Decor and styling

Concept boards, florals, lighting and table settings that carry one clear look from the entrance to the last corner of the room.

On the day coordination

How we work

A clear process, from first call to final toast

Every event moves through the same four stages, so nothing is left to chance and you always know what happens next.

Discovery call

We learn your date, guest count, budget and the feeling you want the day to leave behind.

Concept and budget

You receive a design concept, vendor shortlist and a line by line budget before anything is booked.

Booking and build

We confirm vendors, build the run of show and handle every follow up in the weeks before the event.

Event day

Our coordination team runs the room from setup to breakdown, so you are a guest at your own event.
