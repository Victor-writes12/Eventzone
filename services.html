What we offer

Every service your event needs, coordinated by one team

Book one service or the full package; each one is built to fit into a single, well run event day.

Weddings

Traditional, white, or both in one weekend. We manage the guest list, vendor contracts, budget and run of show, so your family gets to enjoy the day instead of running it.

Corporate events

Product launches, conferences, staff parties and AGMs, delivered on brand and on time, with a dedicated run of show your internal team can rely on.

Social celebrations

Children's Birthdays, naming and graduation events, styled to feel personal; from an intimate garden lunch to a 300 guest hall.

Adult celebrations

Milestone birthdays such as 30th, 40th, 50th and 60th parties, plus retirement send offs and anniversary dinners; planned with the same detail as a wedding, at any scale.

Decor and styling

Concept boards, floral direction, lighting design and table settings, carrying one consistent look from the entrance through to the last corner of the venue.

Vendor management

We source, negotiate with and brief caterers, photographers, decorators and crews, then hold each one accountable to the shared timeline.

On the day coordination

A lead coordinator plus crew on site from setup to breakdown, tracking the timeline in real time so you can stay present as a guest.
